Cookie Policy

Last updated: 18, September, 2026

1. Do you need cookie consent?

As shipped, this site sets no cookies of its own beyond an optional on-device flag that remembers your cookie-banner choice (stored in your browser's local storage, not a tracking cookie). If you add analytics (e.g. Google Analytics, Meta Pixel), embedded video, or advertising scripts, those typically do set cookies and under GDPR (EU/UK), Nigeria's NDPA/NDPR, and similar laws elsewhere, you generally need prior opt-in consent before loading anything non-essential. That's why this site ships with a cookie banner that blocks non-essential scripts until you click "Accept all."

2. Categories of cookies

Category Purpose Consent needed?
Essential Remembering your cookie preference; basic site function. No strictly necessary.
Analytics (if added) Understanding how visitors use the site, in aggregate. Yes.
Advertising (if added) Measuring or targeting ads. Yes.

3. How consent works on this site

When you first visit, a banner asks you to accept or reject non-essential cookies. Your choice is remembered on your device.

4. Managing cookies

You can change your cookie choice at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this site, or by adjusting your browser's cookie settings directly. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their privacy settings.
